# User stories

Eleven stories for the studio as it works today. Each one names who is acting: **teacher**, **parent**, or **child**. The child is the student who signs in with the name the teacher wrote and a PIN.

A story is done when its definition of success holds.

## 1. One tap after class

**Profile:** Teacher

As a teacher, I want to record that a lesson happened from Today, so that the practice is up to date before I pack up.

**Definition of success**

- Today lists the people I teach, and each row has one lesson action.
- One tap writes a lesson for today, using that person's usual subject, platform, and place.
- The row shows that it was saved.
- A prepaid pack counts one lesson fewer. A per-lesson price adds one lesson to the open balance.
- The same date is visible to the linked parent and to the child, even before materials or homework are filled in.

## 2. Write what we studied

**Profile:** Teacher

As a teacher, I want to add the materials and the homework to a lesson, so that the family and the child are looking at the same page I am.

**Definition of success**

- From the person's page I can save a lesson with a date, subject, platform, place, materials, and homework.
- That lesson appears in their history.
- The child sees the homework under what they have to do, and the materials in the lesson history.
- The linked parent sees the same materials and homework, and cannot change them.
- I can remove a lesson I saved by mistake, and the counts update.

## 3. Know who to follow up

**Profile:** Teacher

As a teacher, I want one place that shows open balances and packs running low, so that I follow up while it is still a small thing.

**Definition of success**

- Money lists people with an open balance, and people on a prepaid pack with two lessons left, one left, none left, or lessons past the pack.
- Each row names the person and the status, and opens their page.
- People who are settled, and packs with lessons to spare, stay off this page.
- When nobody needs a follow-up, the page says everyone is up to date.

## 4. Write down a payment

**Profile:** Teacher

As a teacher, I want to record money I have received, so that the balance or the pack matches what was actually paid.

**Definition of success**

- On the person's page I can save a date and an amount.
- For a prepaid pack I can also say how many lessons that payment adds. Those lessons increase what is left.
- For a per-lesson price, the open balance falls by the amount. Paying more than is owed shows as credit.
- The payment stays in the history. I can remove it, and the balance or the pack returns to the previous count.
- An empty payment, with no amount and no lessons added, is refused.

## 5. Bring someone in from the notebooks

**Profile:** Teacher

As a teacher, I want to add a person I already teach, so that four years of paper can move into the studio without waiting for a new signup.

**Definition of success**

- I can save a name, whether they are a child or an adult, a subject, a platform, a place, a price, and either per-lesson or prepaid billing.
- They appear on Today with that status.
- If I enter money already received, or lessons already in a pack, that opening payment is on their record.
- If I enter a parent's name and email, that parent is linked and can sign in to this child only.
- I can set a PIN of four to six digits, or clear it. The child uses that PIN with their name.

## 6. Ask for a place

**Profile:** Parent

As a parent, I want to tell the teacher about my child, so that we can start without my details being published first.

**Definition of success**

- I can send my name and email, my child's name, and the subject, with school, grade, interests, and ambitions if I have them.
- I am told the registration was sent, and that I can sign in only after the teacher confirms it.
- The teacher sees it waiting, with the parent and the child named. It does not create a lesson record or a sign-in on its own.
- An email that is already in use is refused.

## 7. Open or close a signup

**Profile:** Teacher

As a teacher, I want to accept or decline a registration, so that only families I have confirmed can see lessons.

**Definition of success**

- Signups shows only registrations still waiting.
- Accept creates the child, links that parent, and takes me to the new page so I can set a price. The parent can then sign in and see that child.
- Decline drops it from the waiting list and creates no child, no parent sign-in, and no lessons.
- When nothing is waiting, the page says so.

## 8. Read the same lessons

**Profile:** Parent

As a parent, I want to read what was studied and what the homework is, so that I am not reconstructing the week from messages.

**Definition of success**

- After I sign in I see only the children linked to me, with subject, place, and whether the pack or the balance needs attention.
- Opening a child shows each lesson's date, subject, platform, place, materials, and homework, as the teacher wrote them.
- I can look, and I can leave feedback. I cannot edit or delete a lesson.
- If the teacher has saved a phone number, I can call it from the page. If not, the page says the number is not there yet.

## 9. See my homework

**Profile:** Child

As a child, I want to open my own lessons, so that I know what to do and what we already covered.

**Definition of success**

- I sign in with my name, as the teacher wrote it, and the PIN they gave me. A wrong pair does not open anyone else's page.
- I see my name, the homework still written on recent lessons, and the full list of my lessons with materials.
- If nothing has been written yet, the page says so, rather than showing an empty account that looks broken.
- I do not see payments, other children, or notes my parent left.

## 10. Say how the lessons feel

**Profile:** Parent

As a parent, I want to tell the teacher how the teaching feels, so that a worry or a thanks is on the record instead of in a side chat.

**Definition of success**

- I can leave a general note, about one child or about the lessons overall.
- If the teacher has asked me a question, I can answer it with a score from 1 to 5 for pace, following the material, homework clarity, and overall feeling, plus a written answer.
- After I send it, I can see the note I left, and the question shows as answered.
- I can only write about children linked to me.

## 11. Hear it, and ask when I need to

**Profile:** Teacher

As a teacher, I want to read what parents have said and ask one clear question when I need to, so that feedback is part of the practice and not a thread I lose.

**Definition of success**

- Feedback shows notes and answers with the parent's name and, when it is about one child, that child's name.
- An answer includes the four scores and the written reply, and a request shows whether it is still waiting or already answered.
- From a child's page I can send one open question to a linked parent. They see it the next time they open feedback.
- If no parent is linked, I am told to link one before I can ask.
